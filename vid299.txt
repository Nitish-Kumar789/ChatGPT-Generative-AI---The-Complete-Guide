In this module, we learned about:

RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation)
CAG (Context-Augmented Generation)
Few-Shot Prompting
Fine-Tuning

All of these techniques are used to improve the performance of Large Language Models (LLMs), but they do so in different ways.

RAG/CAG provide external knowledge to the model at inference time.
Few-Shot Prompting teaches the model how to perform a task using examples in the prompt.
Fine-Tuning modifies the model's weights through additional training to specialize its behavior.

These approaches are related because they all help adapt a base model to specific requirements, but they operate at different stages and have different trade-offs in terms of cost, complexity, and flexibility.
