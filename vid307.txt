In AI Automation, there is a predefined workflow consisting of a series of repetitive tasks.

The workflow follows a fixed sequence of steps.
It can be triggered manually, on a schedule, or by an event (e.g., receiving an email or form submission).
Behind the scenes, one or more AI agents or LLMs may be used to perform specific tasks.
The overall process remains workflow-driven and predictable.
AI automation is best suited for repetitive business processes and routine operations.
