What is AutoGPT?

AutoGPT is an autonomous AI agent framework where the user primarily defines a goal, and the agent attempts to plan and execute the steps needed to achieve that goal.

The user provides a high-level objective.
AutoGPT breaks the objective into smaller tasks.
It can reason about what actions to take next.
It can use tools to gather information and perform tasks.
Depending on its configuration, it may have access to the internet, files, APIs, databases, or other external tools.
It can execute multiple steps automatically with limited human intervention.
Example

Goal:

"Research the latest AI trends and create a summary report."

AutoGPT may:

Search for recent AI news.
Collect relevant information.
Summarize the findings.
Generate a report.
Key Idea

Traditional automation: You define the steps.

AutoGPT: You primarily define the goal, and the agent attempts to determine and execute the steps required to achieve it.**
