Building Automated AI Workflows

In traditional automation, we usually define the exact steps that need to be executed.

In AI-powered workflows and agents, we often focus more on defining the goal, while the AI helps determine the actions needed to achieve that goal.

In this section, we will explore:

Auto-GPT for goal-oriented AI agents.
LangChain for building custom AI workflows and agents.

Topics may include:

Defining goals and tasks for AI agents.
Connecting AI models with tools and external services.
Building multi-step workflows.
Automating complex processes using AI.
Important Note

"Here we have to just define the goal, not the steps"

This is partially true for autonomous agents such as Auto-GPT, where the agent can plan intermediate steps itself. However, in many AI workflow frameworks (including LangChain), developers still define some or all of the workflow logic, tools, and constraints.

Simple Summary

In this section, we will learn how to build AI-powered workflows and agents. We will explore Auto-GPT for goal-driven automation and LangChain for creating custom AI workflows that combine LLMs, tools, memory, and external services.
