Using AutoGPT for AI Automation

AutoGPT can be used to automate tasks by defining a goal and allowing the agent to plan and execute actions toward that goal.
It can perform multi-step tasks using available tools, internet access, files, and other resources (depending on configuration).
AutoGPT may request user approval or manual intervention before executing certain actions, especially when confirmation is required.
By observing how AutoGPT plans and executes tasks, users can gain a better understanding of how AI agents operate.

Small Correction:

"you can easily see thoughts, reasoning, plan, criticism"

Modern AI systems generally do not expose their full internal chain of thought or reasoning process. However, AutoGPT may display:

Goals
Plans
Proposed actions
Self-evaluation or reflections
Task progress

These are summaries of the agent's process rather than its complete internal reasoning.

Simple Summary

AutoGPT allows users to automate multi-step tasks by providing a goal. It can create plans, perform actions, use tools, and sometimes request manual approval. Watching its planning and execution process can help users understand how AI agents work.
