AI Automation vs AI Agents

AI Automation
Used for simpler, predefined, and repetitive tasks.
AI involvement is limited.
Follows fixed workflows and rules.
Example: Auto-replying to emails or summarizing documents.
AI Agents
Used for complex and dynamic tasks.
AI involvement is much higher.
Goal-oriented and capable of making decisions.
Can plan, reason, and use tools to achieve an objective.
Example: Researching a topic, analyzing findings, and generating a report.
One-line Summary

AI Automation is workflow-based, whereas AI Agents are goal-based.
